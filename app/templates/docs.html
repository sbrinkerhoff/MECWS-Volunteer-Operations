Volunteer Guide

Welcome to the MECWS Operations Portal. this guide will help you understand how to use the system to manage your volunteer shifts.

Contents
  • 1. Logging In (Magic Links)
  • 2. The Dashboard
  • 3. Managing Your Schedule
  • 4. Email Notifications

1. Logging In

We use a passwordless "Magic Link" system for security and ease of use.

  1. Go to the Login Page.
  2. Enter your email address.
  3. Check your email inbox for a message from MECWS Ops.
  4. Click the unique login link in the email.
Only registered volunteers can log in. If your email is not recognized, please contact a supervisor.

2. The Dashboard

Once logged in, you will see your Dashboard. This is your home base.

  • My Schedule: View your upcoming assigned shifts.
  • Available Shifts: Browse the calendar and sign up for open slots.

3. Managing Your Schedule

Viewing Your Shifts

Click on My Schedule to see a list of shifts you are assigned to. Each shift shows the date, time, and your confirmation status.

Confirmed: You are fully set for this shift.
Pending: Your signup is awaiting supervisor approval.

Signing Up

Go to Available Shifts to see a calendar of open nights. Click on a shift to sign up. You can choose to be "Available" (Pending) or, if configured, confirm immediately.

4. Email Notifications

You will receive emails for:

  • Login Links
  • Shift Confirmations
  • Nightly Schedules: Sent out before the shift starts, detailing who is on duty.

Need help? Contact your Shelter Supervisor.
